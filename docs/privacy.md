# Privacy (v1)

Dose Segura v1 is offline-first and does **not** enable product analytics.

- No account required for core offline use
- Medication catalog and procedure templates are bundled locally
- Favorites, theme, and user procedures stay in on-device storage
- No Google Analytics, Plausible, ads, or other product trackers in the app
- Web hosting (e.g. GitHub Pages) may still produce infrastructure access logs outside app control

In-app copy: Settings → Legal → Privacy (`app/privacy.tsx`). Contact: `pmrobalo@gmail.com`.
